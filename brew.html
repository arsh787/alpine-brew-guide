Mastering the Pour-Over

Brewing exceptional coffee at home starts with quality beans, precise water temperature (around 96 °C), and consistent grind size. Take your time and enjoy the ritual.

Step-by-Step Method

Use a 1:16 ratio—25 g of medium-fine coffee to 400 g of filtered water. Allow the grounds to bloom for 30 seconds before continuing your pour in steady, concentric circles.

French Press Immersion

For a heavier body and rich oils, steep coarse grounds for four minutes in a 1:15 ratio. Break the surface crust gently before pressing down slowly.

Roaster's Note

Always grind your beans right before brewing. Freshly ground coffee unlocks volatile aromatics that pre-ground coffee loses in minutes.
